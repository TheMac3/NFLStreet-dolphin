Why GPL-2.0-or-later
--------------------

The rollback netcode lives in a fork of the Dolphin emulator, which is
GPLv2+, so that half has no other option. The launcher and tools here are a
separate program - they run Dolphin as a subprocess and link none of its
code - but they are vendored into that public fork and shipped in the same
archive, so they carry the same license rather than leaving a reader to guess
which terms apply to which file.

Fonts and generated art
-----------------------

Ubuntu Sans remains under the Ubuntu Font Licence 1.0 and its source TTF is
unmodified. The lossless WOFF2 container used by the launcher UI is renamed
"Ubuntu Sans derivative NSRB" as that licence requires for a modified
container; glyphs, hinting and variation data are unchanged. Its copyright and
licence are in assets/branding/UBUNTU-FONT-LICENSE.txt, and archived DejaVu
notices remain in assets/branding/FONT-LICENSE.txt. Fonts keep their own
licences independently of the compositions generated from them. The procedural
background textures are original, with no third-party image input.
